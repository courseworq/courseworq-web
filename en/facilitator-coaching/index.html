Service

Facilitator coaching

Sometimes what you need isn't for us to design for you, but to work alongside you while you design and facilitate. We work with you so your own sessions land with confidence.

Get a quote See all services

Formats

  • 1:1 sessions to review your session design with the Session Design Canvas.
  • Rehearsals with concrete feedback on structure, pacing, voice and group management.
  • Workshops for in-house facilitator teams.
  • Multi-week incubator programs to prepare a talk or workshop from start to finish.
  • Written feedback on your materials before you present them.

Who it's for

  • New facilitators
  • Speakers preparing their first talk
  • In-house facilitator teams
  • Experts who need to teach what they know

Example

From a talk idea to the stage

An illustrative scenario of how we work
  1. The challenge

    An expert wants to give their first conference talk and doesn't know where to start.

  2. What we design

    A few weeks working together: turning the idea into a clear promise with the Session Design Canvas, building the structure and key moments, and two rehearsals with feedback on pacing, voice, opening and closing.

  3. How we know it worked

    They walk on stage with a talk that is truly theirs and know exactly what they want people to take away.
